Workflow

From the stain to an AI-assisted read

Standardize, digitize, and interpret within your existing workflow. The software is for research use only, not for use in diagnostic procedures.

1

Run Control IHC Staining

Cut and stain the control lines with the patient's tissue, on the same glass and in the same run.

2

Upload to the Platform

The laboratory uploads the H&E and p53 slides to the platform. The three control lines are already on the p53 slide.

3

Review and Sign

The pathologist reviews the grades and signs. Every tumor nucleus is graded against the slide's own controls, and the report is one page.
