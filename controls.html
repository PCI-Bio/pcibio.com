Run Quality

A Weak Run Can Look Like Lost Expression

An IHC stain depends on many steps, from fixation and antigen retrieval to antibody concentration and detection. If one of them drifts:

Result: staining looks weaker than it should, or absent. A control line of known expression, stained in the same run, shows it.

Reproducible IHC with Known-Expression Controls

In immunohistochemistry (IHC), the read depends on the stain. HER2 scores select patients for HER2-targeted therapy; mismatch-repair deficiency and PD-L1 scores are used to select patients for checkpoint-inhibitor therapy.

Tissue controls are standard practice. Tissue controls vary, and null-pattern tissue is hard to find; a cell line gives the same known pattern every time. Stained in the same run, a cell-line control of known expression shows whether the run stained as expected.

Material Formats

Tailored for Every Validation Workflow

Choose between primary blocks or pre-cut slides to match your throughput.

Format A

FFPE Blocks

Formalin-Fixed Paraffin-Embedded cellular block containing homogeneous cell plugs spanning the entire block depth. Highly scalable for high-volume pathology institutions.

  • • Yields 150 - 200 cuts per block
  • • Cut at 4-5 microns per lab's microtome
  • • Excellent for custom antibody development

BEST FOR:

Validation, customized assays, high volume runs.

Format B

Pre-Cut Slides

Pre-mounted 4-micron thick cellular cuts placed on charged silicate adhesive glass. Placed on the bottom section of grease-free glass, leaving the rest of the slide free for the tissue section.

  • • Pre-baked, direct stain ready
  • • 18 months room-temperature shelf-life
  • • No microtome cuts equipment needed

BEST FOR:

Staining-run checks, quality reviews, training classes.

Calibration Layouts

The Molecular Gradient Spectrum

Our products organize distinct cell plugs next to each other, creating visual and quantitative density steps.

TriControl™ Staining Thresholds Spectrum (p53 Example)

OE

Overexpression line

Strong nuclear staining (3+)

WT

Wild-type line

Variable, weak to moderate nuclear staining

NULL

Null line

No nuclear staining (0)

Each plug holds one characterized cell line. A pathologist, or research software such as PCI AI, reads the three plugs side by side to check the stain across its range. Overexpression and null are the abnormal patterns that point to a TP53 mutation.

Biomarker Main Use Staining and Patterns PCI Catalog Codes
p53 Classifying endometrial and ovarian carcinomas, as a surrogate for TP53 mutation. Nuclear. Overexpression, wild-type or null; overexpression and null are the abnormal patterns. Cytoplasmic staining is a rarer abnormal pattern. PCI-58 | PCI-55 + 10 more
MMR: MLH1, PMS2, MSH2, MSH6 Screening for mismatch-repair deficiency and Lynch syndrome in colorectal and endometrial carcinoma. Nuclear, read as intact or lost. Which proteins are lost points to the affected gene. PCI-02 | PCI-01 + 15 more
PD-L1 Selecting patients for checkpoint-inhibitor therapy. Membranous, partial or complete. The score (TPS or CPS) and its cut-off depend on the antibody clone and the indication. PCI-57 | PCI-59 | PCI-56 | PCI-40
HER2 Breast and gastric carcinoma; selects patients for HER2-targeted therapy. Membranous, scored 0 to 3+. PCI-33 | PCI-34
p16 Surrogate for high-risk HPV in cervical and oropharyngeal carcinoma. Block-type (strong, diffuse nuclear and cytoplasmic) versus patchy staining. PCI-15 | PCI-00 + 8 more
PTEN Endometrial carcinoma, among others. Loss of expression in tumor cells, with retained staining in internal controls. PCI-12 | PCI-25 | PCI-06 | PCI-37 | PCI-46
BRAF V600E Colorectal carcinoma, melanoma, thyroid carcinoma. Mutation-specific antibody (clone VE1): cytoplasmic staining in tumor cells that carry the mutation. PCI-13 | PCI-16 | PCI-48
MYC A control line for IHC assay development and antibody validation. Nuclear. MYC-amplified (positive) control line. PCI-45
PR A control line for IHC assay development and antibody validation. Nuclear. Negative control line. PCI-32

FOLR1 Control lines for IHC assay development and antibody validation. Membranous. Overexpression (positive) and negative control lines. PCI-38 | PCI-39
Beta-catenin A control line for IHC assay development and antibody validation. Membranous; nuclear staining is the abnormal pattern. Single control line. PCI-47 | PCI-04 | PCI-14
